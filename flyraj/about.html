Our Story

✦

Flyraj Tour & Travels Ltd. was founded in 2010 with a singular mission: to make world-class travel accessible, affordable, and spiritually meaningful for the people of Bangladesh.

What began as a small Umrah facilitation service in Dhaka has grown into one of Bangladesh's most respected travel agencies — offering Umrah & Hajj pilgrimages, international tours, visa processing, and airline ticket services.

We are proud to be ATAB (Association of Travel Agents of Bangladesh) registered and fully government-licensed, trusted by over 15,000 travellers and pilgrims.
